I am a Clinical Research Specialist at Nobel İlaç with extensive experience in BE/BA studies, clinical trials, and regulatory compliance. I specialize in managing preclinical and clinical activities, ensuring compliance with R&D timelines and product launches.

With an MSc in Medical Biotechnology (GPA 4.0/4.0) and currently pursuing a PhD in Gene and Cell Therapy at Akdeniz University, I combine pharmaceutical expertise with strong research capabilities. My background includes managing regulatory submissions in over 50 countries, negotiating contracts with CROs across Europe, USA, and India, and preparing clinical and non-clinical CTD modules.

My research interests include machine learning applications in drug discovery and advanced therapeutics. I have published several papers on topics ranging from anticancer peptides to SARS-CoV-2 protein modeling.

Throughout my career, I have successfully:

- Negotiated contracts with over 10 CROs while establishing partnerships with various service vendors
- Prepared clinical modules for more than 30 medicinal products including biosimilars
- Developed RPA software to track study timelines and regulatory applications
- Conducted monitoring visits to clinical trials and BE studies
- Managed pharmacovigilance activities within clinical trials

I'm passionate about the intersection of pharmaceutical research, data science, and advanced therapeutics.
